# Banoffee Pie Recipe

## Ingredients
- 4 bananas, sliced
- 394g caramel or dulce de leche
- 300ml double cream
- Dark chocolate (optional)

### For the pastry
- 100g chilled butter (plus extra for greasing)
- 200g plain flour
- 1 medium egg, separated
- 1 tbsp golden caster sugar

## Method

1. Make the pastry case: Pulse butter and flour in a food processor until breadcrumb texture. Add egg yolk and sugar, pulse until combined. Add cold water gradually until dough forms.
2. Knead dough gently, wrap in cling film and chill for 30 minutes.
3. Heat oven to 190C/170C fan/gas 4. Grease a 23cm loose-bottomed tart tin. Roll out pastry and line the tin, leaving 0.5cm overhang.
4. Line pastry with baking parchment and weights (beans or rice). Bake for 15 mins. Remove weights, brush surface with egg white, bake for another 15-20 mins until golden. Cool completely.
5. Spread half the caramel on the pastry case, layer bananas on top, cover with remaining caramel, and chill.
6. Whip cream until thick and spoon over tart. Grate dark chocolate over to garnish if liked.

This recipe serves 8-10 and is rated easy with a total time of about 1 hour.
